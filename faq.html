Frequently asked questions

How long is the waiting list?

Most young people wait four to seven months to be matched. Boys usually wait longer because we have fewer male mentors.

Can my child join?

Young people ages 8 to 17 who live or go to school in Cedar Hollow County can join. A parent or guardian fills out an enrollment form and we schedule a short interview. There is no cost to families.

Can couples mentor together?

Yes. Couples can be matched with one young person as a couple match. Both partners complete the full screening.

Do I need a car?

No, but community mentors need a way to get to outings. School-based mentors only need to get to the school.

What if the match does not work out?

Your match support specialist works with you, the young person and their family. If a match needs to close, we close it with care and a final goodbye meeting.
