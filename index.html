Every kid deserves someone in their corner

Cedar Hollow Community Mentors matches young people ages 8 to 17 with trained adult volunteers for one-to-one mentoring. We have served families in Cedar Hollow County since 2011.

What we do

  • One-to-one mentoring in the community and at school
  • Teen Futures, a college and career program for ages 14 to 17
  • Family Nights every month for mentors, mentees and their families

Get involved

We have 42 young people on our waiting list right now. Become a mentor, make a gift, or come to an event.
