Support a match

It costs about $1,200 a year to support one mentoring match, covering background checks, training and match support.

  • $50 covers one mentor background check
  • $100 pays for a Family Night dinner
  • $1,200 supports a match for a full year

Give online. You can also mail a check to our office at the address on our contact page.
