Our programs

Community mentoring

Mentors and mentees meet for at least four hours a month for a minimum of one year. Matches choose their own activities: a library visit, a pickup basketball game, cooking a meal together. Each match has a dedicated match support specialist who checks in monthly.

School-based mentoring

Mentors meet their mentee once a week during lunch at one of our three partner elementary schools: Oak Ridge Elementary, Mill Creek Elementary and Hollow Springs Elementary. The commitment is one school year.

Teen Futures

For mentees ages 14 to 17. Teen Futures adds two group workshops a month on applying to college, trade school and first jobs, plus a campus visit each spring.

Family Nights

On the second Thursday of every month we host a free dinner for matches and their families at our office, from 6:00 to 7:30 pm.

Cedar Hollow Community Mentors is a fictional organization. This site exists only to test software; it is not a real charity and accepts no donations or sign-ups.
