Become a mentor

Mentors are adults age 21 or older who can commit to a full year. You do not need any special background; we train you.

Requirements

  • Age 21 or older
  • A criminal background check and a driving record check (we pay for both)
  • Three personal references
  • A 90-minute in-person interview
  • Three hours of pre-match training

How to apply

Start your application on our volunteer application form. The whole process, from application to your first meeting, usually takes six to eight weeks.

Questions? Call our volunteer coordinator at (512) 555-0147.
